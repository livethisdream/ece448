    An Applications Course

    Watch what the signal does; don’t calculate it. We use real radios and GNU Radio to observe effects, such as what happens when the noise goes up, and, when it helps, tie what we see back to theory.

    ECE 447 is where communications theory is derived. Here we use it: every lesson starts with a signal to build, receive, or decode, and brings in the theory only when the flowgraph needs it.

    Course Goals

    By the end of this course you should be able to:

    1. Simulate, receive, and transmit signals with modern software and modulation techniques.

    2. Describe filtering, interpolation, decimation, and matched filtering.

    3. Apply them to real signals, noise and non-idealities included.

    4. Reverse-engineer, decode, and characterize an unknown RF signal.

    5. Define, execute, and defend your own SDR project.

    How This Course Is Graded

    Every learning objective is scored on a binary 1 / 0 scale, with no partial credit. A 0 comes with detailed feedback rather than the answer, and if you submitted real effort on time you may revise your work until you earn the 1.
