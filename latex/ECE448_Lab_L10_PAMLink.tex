\fullwidth{\textbf{Learning Objective 4.4: } I can implement a PAM link that transmits and
receives ASCII text end-to-end.}

\textbf{Instructions:} Submit this packet as one complete submission by the date indicated on
Gradescope. This is an individual assignment, but you may collaborate, with appropriate
documentation. Bring your PlutoSDR, a short SMA cable, and the 30 dB SMA attenuator. Steps 1
to 10 run on your laptop and transmit nothing. Steps 11 to 15 transmit from your PlutoSDR's
TX port, through the cable and the attenuator, to its own RX port, at 915 MHz; connect the
cable and attenuator before you run the flowgraph, and never connect an antenna in this lab.

\textbf{Part 1: Predict} the shaded entries in Table A before the lab period, attaching your
work.

\textbf{Part 2: Measure} the indicated quantities in class, and paste Figures 1 to 3.

\textbf{Part 3: Analyze} the results according to the prompts in this section, after the lab.

\textbf{Grading:} I grade the packet against Objective 4.4 on the course's 1 / 0 scale, and you
may revise it under course policy. I grade each Part 3 answer on its claim, the evidence from
your own table and figures, the reasoning that connects them, and the uncertainty in your
readings.
