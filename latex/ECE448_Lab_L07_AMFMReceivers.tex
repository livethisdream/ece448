\fullwidth{\textbf{Learning Objective 3.3: } I can implement an FM receiver in GNU
Radio, demodulate a live broadcast FM signal, and decode its RDS data.
\\[2pt]\textbf{Learning Objective 3.4: } I can explain and demonstrate the
trade-off between bandwidth and noise performance for AM vs.\ FM.}

\vspace{2pt}

This packet is the L\labLesson\ Lab, in three parts, and you turn it in once, complete,
before Lesson 8. Do \textbf{Part 1} on your own before the lab period: work out the
receiver settings you will need. \textbf{Part 2} is your own
measurements and observations, on your own laptop and RTL-SDR, recorded in class. Do
\textbf{Part 3} on your own after the lab: explain what you saw.

\textbf{Grading.} We grade one submission against Objectives 3.3 and 3.4 on the
course's 1 / 0 scale, and you may revise it under course policy. Paste screenshots
where a question asks for one.

\textbf{Receive only.} Every flowgraph in this lab receives or simulates. The RTL-SDR
cannot transmit, and nothing in this lab needs a license. Start every receiver with
the volume low.

\textbf{Files.} Download the six L7 flowgraphs from the L7 lesson page into one
folder, with your \texttt{fm\_capture.cfile} from the L5 Lab. Open
\texttt{L07-rds-rx.grc} first and confirm that GRC shows no missing blocks: gr-rds
comes with radioconda; if GRC reports the RDS blocks missing, run
\texttt{mamba install gnuradio-rds} from the radioconda prompt. Then, before the
period, run \texttt{L07-fm-test-tx.grc}, which also uses an RDS block, and
\texttt{L07-am-test-tx.grc} once each; they write \texttt{L07-fm-test.cfile} and
\texttt{L07-am-test.cfile} into that folder and stop on their own.

\question \textbf{Build the WBFM receiver.} Start GRC with an empty canvas. Do not
open \texttt{L07-wbfm-rx.grc}: build the receiver yourself.
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
	\item \texttt{samp\_rate} 2.4e6, a QT GUI Entry \texttt{fc\_mhz} (100.0), a QT GUI
	Entry \texttt{station\_mhz} (100.3), and a QT GUI Range \texttt{vol} from 0 to 1.
	\item A File Source on \texttt{L07-fm-test.cfile}, repeat on.
	\item A Frequency Xlating FIR Filter, decimation 5, center frequency
	\texttt{(station\_mhz - fc\_mhz) * 1e6}, with a Low-pass Filter Taps variable
	\texttt{chan\_taps}: cutoff 105 kHz, transition 30 kHz, at \texttt{samp\_rate}.
	\item A Quadrature Demod with the gain of Question 1(b).
	\item A Decimating FIR Filter, decimation 10, with taps
	\texttt{firdes.low\_pass(1, samp\_rate/5, 15e3, 3e3)}.
	\item FM Deemphasis at 48 kHz with $\tau$ = 75~$\mu$s, a Multiply Const
	\texttt{vol}, an Audio Sink at 48 kHz, and a float QT GUI Frequency Sink on the
	de-emphasis output. Do not add a Throttle.
\end{itemize}

\begin{parts}

	\part Paste a screenshot of your flowgraph on the GRC canvas. Every connection
	must be black, not red.
		\begin{solution}[1.6in]\keysol{sol4}\end{solution}

	\part Type 99.5 into Station. Describe what you hear, and explain why the station
